About Alpha Kappa Rho

53 years of brotherhood, leadership, and service

Our Legacy

Alpha Kappa Rho was founded on August 8, 1973, with a vision to create an organization that would produce leaders who are not only academically excellent but also socially responsible. For 53 years, we have grown from a small group of idealistic students to one of the most respected fraternities in the country.

The BLK 28 Chapter proudly continues this legacy, upholding the values of brotherhood, scholarship, and service in everything we do. Our journey has been marked by challenges and triumphs, but through it all, the bonds of brotherhood have remained unbreakable.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To develop men and women of character who will serve as leaders in their communities, excel in their chosen fields, and uphold the values of brotherhood, scholarship, and service throughout their lives.

Vision

To be the premier student organization recognized for producing transformative leaders who make lasting positive impacts on society through innovation, compassion, and unwavering integrity.
